# ctSpaces 6.0.3.0

## Upgrade-oriented guide markers

The 20-step Quick tour and its 12 inert illustrations are unchanged from
6.0.2.0. This release corrects the upgrade framing for colleagues whose
supplied last-shipped artifact was
5.2.0.14 and gives the eight relevant What's-new topics their dedicated
change-only titles:

- **Firefox & browser slots**
- **Reorder open tabs**
- **Links, shortcuts & ordering**
- **Client-first window titles**
- **Rename & archive clients**
- **Two bulk-delete tools**
- **Improved themed popups**
- **Guides & visual tour**

These markers describe accumulated post-baseline guidance; they do not claim
that every capability first appeared in 6.0.3.0. Session restoration,
pinning, themes, backup/restore, and other 5.2 basics remain documented but are
not relabelled as newly introduced. What's New uses separate titles and bodies
instead of repeating full manual chapters. The old Temporary/Default chapter
no longer gets a New marker. The tour never reads the clipboard, creates
clients, launches browsers, reorders state, or performs cleanup/deletion.

The supplied baseline artifact is documented in
[`docs/BASELINE_5.2.0.14.md`](docs/BASELINE_5.2.0.14.md). No claim is made here
that the baseline executable was executed or fully compared at runtime.
The normal runtime ZIP remains app-only and contains exactly `ctSpaces.exe`.
